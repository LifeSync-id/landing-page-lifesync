## Brand & Style

This design system embodies **Warm Minimalism & Mindful Productivity**. It bridges intentional personal organization with tactile warmth, stripping away the cold, hyper-metricized anxiety of conventional productivity apps. Designed for introspective, design-conscious individuals seeking a unified ecosystem—habits, daily planning, macro nutrition, personal finance, rich markdown notes, and visual travel memories—it replaces cognitive clutter with breathing room, serene pacing, and organic visual stability.

The aesthetic philosophy balances Japanese and Scandinavian minimalism (Japandi) with modern mobile craftsmanship:
- **Quiet Clarity:** Content and personal data take center stage against calm, paper-inspired substrates. Visual noise is actively eliminated; whitespace functions as an active structural element rather than void.
- **Organic & Grounded Tactility:** Elements mimic layered, high-grade textured stationery. Subtle borders and earth-tinted drops shadows convey gentle spatial layers without aggressive digital elevation.
- **Intentional Feedback:** Micro-interactions, completed habits, and scanned receipts/nutrition tags resolve with gentle warmth (soft terracotta pings and calming sage blooms) rather than gamified, frantic bursts.

## Colors

The palette draws deeply from raw ceramics, weathered paper, botanical tones, and sunlit terracotta. It avoids stark pure blacks and cold sterile whites in favor of low-stress warmth and high legibility.

### Surface Tiers & Canvas
- **Base Canvas (Light Screen Ground):** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream). Softens eye strain and feels distinctly paper-like.
- **Secondary Canvas (Groupings & Backgrounds):** `#F4EFEB`. Used behind nested carousels, sheet presentations, and list groups.
- **Surface Containers (Default Card Ground):** `#F2ECE4` (Soft Sand), elevating to `#FFFFFF` strictly for focused inputs, popovers, and elevated modal panels.
- **Surface Highlight / Inset:** `#E8E1D7` (Subtle sand outline and divider substrate).

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (Headlines, dense readouts):** `#1F2421` (Earthy Warm Stone Slate). Provides deep, authoritative contrast without harsh digital blackness.
- **Text Secondary (Subtitles, metadata, timestamps):** `#5A605B`.
- **Text Tertiary (Placeholder, disabled labels):** `#8D948E`.

### Accents & Functional Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#4A7C59` - Sage Green):** Habits complete, healthy nutrition AKG targets met, positive financial balances, primary toggles, and confirmed tasks.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E07A5F` - Sunset Coral / Terracotta):** Urgent alerts, daily streak milestones, expense markers, travel pins, and critical calls-to-action.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#5E8C6A` - Soft Olive Sage):** Progress bar fills, secondary chip outlines, nutrition macro indicators (Fiber/Vitamins).

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is selected for all typographic scales to achieve contemporary precision softened by humanist terminals. It reads cleanly in dense financial ledger layouts and macro nutrient trackers, while retaining warmth in introspective journal logs.

- **Numerics:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for habit counts, macro gram counters, and financial values to ensure smooth, un-jittery vertical column scanning.
- **Editorial Pacing:** Journal headers and travel note entries prioritize optical breathing room with comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.6` on body copy).
- **Caps & Hierarchy:** All-caps usage is restricted strictly to `label-sm` (e.g., category eyebrow badges, macro tags like `CARBS`, `PROTEIN`, `KCAL`), set with a slight tracking boost (`letterSpacing: 0.03em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The mobile layout operates on a soft-fluid grid structured around a consistent **4px / 8px base rhythm**.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (Base, `< 640px`):** Single-column stack with `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal safe margins. Content cards stretch across the grid or present in edge-snapping horizontal carousels (e.g., nutrition daily rings, weekly habit rows, travel polaroid journals).
- **Tablet / Expanded (`640px - 1024px`):** 2-column asymmetric split with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1rem` gutters (e.g., Planner / Habit stack on left; Nutrition & Financial telemetry on right).
- **Maximum Device Canvas:** Constrained to `430px` on mobile native viewports, centering gracefully if rendered on wider web containers.

### Safe Areas & Bottom Navigation Clearance
Bottom screen layouts must account for safe area insets and the floating navigation dock. All primary scrolling views append an explicit trailing spacer equal to `space-3xl` + safe area padding (minimum `96px`) to ensure final list items and cards are unobstructed.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids cold, high-altitude drop shadows. Depth is achieved primarily through **tonal layering**, **warm tinted ambient diffusions**, and **tactile hairline borders**.

### Layering Philosophy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` base background.
2. **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** `#F2ECE4` or `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by a 1px border of `#E8E1D7`. Ambient shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Cards, Scanned Items, Floating Actions):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px` border of `rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06)` and shadow `0px 10px 28px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.07)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Nutrition Scanner Viewfinder, Bottom Sheets):** `#FAF8F5` surface with shadow `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.12)`.

### Tactile Edge Detail
All cards feature subtle light borders rather than bare cutouts. In dark accents (like quick-entry chips or primary buttons), subtle internal inset shadows (`inset 0px 1px 0px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) provide a gently bevelled, physical pressable feel.

## Shapes

Corner radiuses reflect organic, tactile pebbles and softened leatherbound notebooks:
- **Base Cards & Modules:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) creates a soft frame for tracking charts, notes previews, and nutrition meal items.
- **Hero Modules & Bottom Sheets:** `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`) for scanner viewfinders, modal pull-up drawers, and photo journal containers.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`).
- **Pills, Interactive Chips, & Badges:** Full continuous pill curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to invite thumb taps and maintain soft physical responsiveness.
- **Nested Inner Components:** Strictly follow the nested corner radius rule: inner radius equals outer radius minus container padding, preventing awkward spatial disharmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log Entry, Scan Complete, Save):** Pill or 16px radius, solid `#4A7C59` (Sage) or `#1F2421` (Stone Slate). High-contrast white text, subtle top inset highlight, hover/press state subtly darkens by 6%.
- **Secondary Action:** `#F2ECE4` background, `1px` border of `#E8E1D7`, text `#1F2421`.
- **Destructive / Urgent Action:** `#E07A5F` background, white text.
- **Height & Touch Target:** Minimum 48px height, 16px horizontal padding.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 32px to 36px.
- **Default State:** `#F2ECE4` surface, `#5A605B` text, `rounded-full`.
- **Selected State:** `#4A7C59` background with `#FAF8F5` text, or `#1F2421` background with white text. Seamlessly toggles habit filters (e.g., "Morning", "Mindfulness", "Finances").

### Habit Matrix & Checkbox Elements
- Custom circular check controls (28px diameter).
- **Unchecked:** Inset ring border of `2px solid #E8E1D7` against cream surface.
- **Checked:** Smooth scale-in spring animation filling with `#4A7C59` and displaying a crisp `#FAF8F5` check icon.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- 52px height, `rounded-lg` (16px).
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFEB` (to contrast with the parent background).
- Border: `1px solid #E8E1D7`. Active focus shifts border to `#4A7C59` with a subtle 3px ring of `rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.15)`.

### Cards (Habit, Finance Ledger, Nutrition, Journal)
- **Habit Card:** Horizontal layout with status ring, habit title, streak count in `#E07A5F` (Terracotta) text, and single-tap check node.
- **Nutrition AKG Card:** Split layout featuring circular ring gauge for daily calories/AKG target percentages alongside micro-tags for Protein, Carbs, and Fats.
- **Travel Gallery Card:** 4:3 or 1:1 rounded image container with a 4px inner paper margin, photo metadata in `label-sm`, and subtle pin accent.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- Floating dock positioned 16px above the home indicator, styled with frosted `#FAF8F5` (90% opacity, 16px backdrop-blur) and a `1px` border of `#E8E1D7`.
- Features 5 primary destinations (Habits, Planner, Nutrition Scanner / Quick Add [Center Accent], Notes & Finance, Travel Journal).
- Center Action Button uses a distinctive `#1F2421` or `#4A7C59` pill floating slightly above the dock plane.
